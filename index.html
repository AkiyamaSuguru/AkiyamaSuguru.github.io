About

I am a master's student in Condensed Matter Physics in a joint program between Shanghai University and the Shanghai Institute of Applied Physics (SINAP), Chinese Academy of Sciences, advised by Prof. Hefei Huang and Prof. Yibing Zhang.

My research concerns SiC coatings on nuclear graphite for molten salt reactors: how the deposition temperature affects their structure and stoichiometry, how they corrode in molten salt, and how they respond to ion irradiation.

I also develop open-source Python tools for processing materials characterization data.

Research

M.S. thesis · Jul 2025 — Present

SiC coatings for molten salt reactors

Shanghai Institute of Applied Physics, CAS · Advisor: Prof. Hefei Huang

In progress

  1. 01 · Completed

    Characterization

    CVD-SiC coatings deposited on nuclear graphite at different temperatures, with pyrolytic carbon (PyC) coatings as a reference, characterized by SEM/EDS, XRD, Raman and XPS to relate deposition temperature to morphology, crystal structure and stoichiometry.

  2. 02 · Published

    Corrosion mechanism

    Static molten-salt corrosion tests on SiC and PyC coatings showed that highly crystalline, moderately C-rich SiC coatings perform best, suggesting a defect-tolerant design strategy. Published in Corrosion Science.

  3. 03 · In progress

    Irradiation

    Ion ranges and displacement damage (dpa) calculated with SRIM and custom Python post-processing to set the irradiation parameters. The irradiation experiments are complete; post-irradiation characterization is in progress.
