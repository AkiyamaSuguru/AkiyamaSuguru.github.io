Preface

I am a master's student in Condensed Matter Physics in a joint program between Shanghai University and the Shanghai Institute of Applied Physics (SINAP), Chinese Academy of Sciences, advised by Prof. Hefei Huang and Prof. Yibing Zhang.

My research concerns SiC coatings on nuclear graphite for molten salt reactors: how the deposition temperature affects their structure and stoichiometry, how they corrode in molten salt, and how they respond to ion irradiation.

Alongside the research I build open-source Python tools for materials characterization data. This book gathers both: the tools, the experiments, and the path between them.

— Jay Ma, Shanghai

Plate I

OpenXPSAnalyzer

Python · Flet · AGPL-3.0

Cross-platform app for post-processing XPS data exported from Avantage. Runs on the web, macOS and Windows.

  • Batch import of Avantage Excel exports; each project saved as one NetCDF-4 file
  • Peak position, height and area; RMSE, MAE and R²
  • Hover charts and multi-panel figures; export at 600 dpi or as interactive HTML

OpenGMA

Python · In development · AGPL-3.0

Open General Material Analyzer: a modular toolkit that converts characterization data from different formats into one standard structure for analysis, plotting, calculation and storage.

  • Readers that convert input formats to a standard data structure
  • Analysis modules for common characterization methods

SiC coatings for molten salt reactors

Shanghai Institute of Applied Physics, CAS · Advisor: Prof. Hefei Huang

In progress

How does a thin ceramic coating keep molten salt out of nuclear graphite? I study CVD-SiC coatings deposited at different temperatures: their structure and stoichiometry, how they corrode in molten salt, and how they respond to ion irradiation.

Chapter II · Research

The Path of the Thesis

  1. 01 · Completed

    Characterization

    CVD-SiC coatings deposited on nuclear graphite at different temperatures, with pyrolytic carbon (PyC) coatings as a reference, characterized by SEM/EDS, XRD, Raman and XPS to relate deposition temperature to morphology, crystal structure and stoichiometry.

  2. 02 · Published

    Corrosion mechanism

    Static molten-salt corrosion tests on SiC and PyC coatings showed that highly crystalline, moderately C-rich SiC coatings perform best, suggesting a defect-tolerant design strategy. Published in Corrosion Science.

  3. 03 · In progress

    Irradiation

    Ion ranges and displacement damage (dpa) calculated with SRIM and custom Python post-processing to set the irradiation parameters. The irradiation experiments are complete; post-irradiation characterization is in progress.
